Type and shape

أهلًا يوسف، ابدأ المشاركة
اختر إجابة واحدة، ثم ثبّت إجابتك. بعد التثبيت لا يمكن تغيير الإجابة.
DisplayNoto Naskh Arabic 700 in the original identity. Noto Sans Arabic in Official and Hybrid, where the product uses Frutiger Arabic (not on Google Fonts, so not embedded here).
Scale40 / 34 / 23 / 20 / 15 / 13 px. Question text 23px at line-height 1.65. Body 15px at 1.7.
Radius8px controls and 12px cards in Original. 4px and 8px in Official and Hybrid. One scale per identity.
DepthBorders carry structure. Shadow only on layers that float: sheets, popovers, the book panel on a phone.
Targets44px primary, 36 to 40px secondary, never below 32px. Status always has an icon and a word, never colour alone.

Motion rules

Pressscale 0.97 to 0.985 on :active, 120 to 140ms, ease-out
Colour changes160ms ease-out. Option, strip cell, chip.
Feedback revealopacity and 6px rise, 220ms, cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)
Question change160ms fade and 6px rise by pointer. No animation when changed by keyboard.
Sheets and popovers240ms and 160ms. Popover scales from its trigger, from 0.96, never from 0.
Phone book pane260ms snap with velocity. Follows the finger with no easing while dragging.
Book opening650ms ease-out, once on start and once on the result. The only authored moment.
Reduced motionEverything collapses to near zero. Opening is instant and the 3D book stops idling.
