# NBC platform proposal research

> [!abstract] Start here
> Our strongest working proposal is a **trustworthy Arabic competition experience with accessible book use, defensible scoring and dependable operation**. This collection prepares us to compete for the platform build. It does not claim a guaranteed first place or an official supplier scorecard.

Public sources checked: **7 September 2026**. Collection completed: **8 September 2026**. Audience: the NBC_web proposal, design and delivery team. Format follows the supplied Obsidian guide: metadata, linked notes, callouts and workflow diagrams.

## The five decisions that matter most

1. **Obtain the actual invitation and evaluation rubric.** The supplied PDF describes student competition operations, not how the builder wins.
2. **Secure the approved book and content rights.** We read the four-page requirements PDF, not the underlying book. The available retail record has an edition/page-count ambiguity.
3. **Make the distinctive rules central to the demo.** Book allowed, previous questions accessible, no answer timer, no time-based tie-break, no correct-answer disclosure after the test.
4. **Resolve identity, fairness and publication policy early.** OTP does not settle identity or enrolment assurance; random distribution does not settle score comparability.
5. **Show proof of quality.** Prepare a complete Arabic journey, controlled scoring/release, report reconciliation, recovery and support evidence.

## What is included

- A map of **all 47 source bullets**, including objectives and repeated obligations.
- Seven comparator perspectives: national-identity, reading and school competitions, a documented education-platform award, and digital-service award criteria.
- Three proposed visual directions, a participant journey, Arabic microcopy and accessibility checks.
- An internal proposal readiness scorecard and an eight-minute demonstration outline.
- Scope questions, evidence gaps, privacy/security considerations, operating and cost drivers.
- A register of **37 consulted sources**, including the supplied PDF, with limited-access evidence identified.
- A byte-preserved local copy of the supplied PDF and a source note.

## Evidence labels

**Requirement** means stated in the supplied PDF. **Verified external fact** means supported by the cited source within its scope. **Recommendation / proposed** means our analysis. **Unknown / unverified** means evidence was missing or insufficient.

> [!warning] Material limits
> No exact public NBC tender, supplier evaluation weights, platform winner, budget, deadline or approved book master was verified. Historical same-title forums are not evidence of previous competition editions. Proposed designs and acceptance scenarios have not been built or tested.

## Recommended next action

Use [[11-Open-Questions-and-Evidence-Gaps]] as the agenda for obtaining the official competition pack and conducting a discovery meeting. Then select the smallest demonstrator that proves the actual evaluation criteria and the unusual rules in the brief.

The research stage produced documents only; no platform code, deployment, registration or external submission was performed.
