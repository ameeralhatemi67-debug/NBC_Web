## What the supplied document establishes

The four-page document is headed **مرئيات أعضاء اللجنة العلمية بشأن المقترحات الفنية والتشغيلية لمنصة مسابقة (الانتماء واللحمة الوطنية) وشروطها**. Its footer states **1448هـ / 2026م**, and displays the branding of **الرئاسة العامة لهيئة الأمر بالمعروف والنهي عن المنكر** and **pv.gov.sa**. This identifies the document’s apparent institutional context. It does not independently establish the procuring legal entity, a tender number, signature, final approval, or a launch date. All four pages were visually read because automatic Arabic extraction corrupted text order. See [[Source-Committee-PDF]].

The platform’s purpose is to spread and make accessible the content of the book **الانتماء واللحمة الوطنية**, provide an interactive awareness and knowledge experience, and administer the competition fairly. The audience includes male and female students in middle school, secondary school and university; government, private and international schools; and accredited universities and colleges. National identity and iqama registration are both specified. Residency and nationality eligibility still require clarification; do not silently restrict registration to Saudi nationals. [[Source-Committee-PDF|Supplied brief, pp. 1 and 3]].

The decisive mechanics are objective questions from the book, random question distribution, backward navigation, book access and downloading during participation, no answer timer, automatic scoring, highest-score winners, no time-based tie-breaking, organizer-defined fair tie handling, and no disclosure of correct answers after the test. See [[02-Requirements-and-Acceptance-Matrix]] for the full requirement map.

## The book: a promising lead, not an approved content master

A Samawy retail entry identifies a same-title book by **عبدالرحمن بن عبدالله بن محمد السند**, ISBN **9786030478606**, with a listed 2024 publication year. Its synopsis says six sections and 66 pages, while structured metadata says 62 pages. The listing was inspected directly. [Samawy book record](https://www.samawy.com/i/1007268/الانتماء-واللحمة-الوطنية).

> [!warning] Content dependency
> We have read the requirements PDF, not the underlying competition book. The approved edition, complete text, chapter structure, errata and redistribution permissions remain unverified. Do not create a scored question bank from a retailer synopsis, related lectures, or an assumed table of contents.

Before content production, obtain the organizer-approved digital file; record title, author, edition, publication details, printed versus digital page numbering and file checksum. Confirm rights to reproduce, download, search, adapt for accessibility and, if later requested, narrate the book. Ask whether the scientific committee supplies questions or expects the builder to commission them.

The 2024 retail metadata should not be described as the book’s first publication. A 2021 news report already mentions teaching from a same-title book by Al-Sanad, but that secondary report does not resolve edition identity. [Sada report, 27 February 2021](https://slaati.com/2021/02/27/p1814149.html).
