# Comparable competitions and documented winners

Parent: [[00-NBC-Research-Index]]

> [!abstract] What transfers to our proposal
> Useful precedents make participation rules, assessment ownership and outcomes understandable. Digital-service awards also value user experience, governance and demonstrated impact. None of the sources below establishes NBC’s supplier-selection rubric or explains why an unknown NBC vendor won.

## Comparison at a glance

| Precedent | Relationship to NBC | Useful transfer | What must not be copied automatically |
|---|---|---|---|
| وطني الحبيب, 2014 | Saudi national-identity student quiz | Trace questions to educational objectives | Historical question count, subject scope and prizes |
| تحدي القراءة العربي, 2024 results | Arabic reading and judging | Explicit rubric and approved award categories | Oral judging, multiple books, shared awards |
| إثراء أقرأ, 2024 report | Cultural engagement through books | Put the reading purpose at the center of the service | Critical essays and stage presentations |
| المهارات الثقافية, first-season archive | Saudi school competition operations | Stage and institution handling; published process | Creative-work judging weights |
| المشروع الوطني للقراءة, Egypt | School-to-university participation | Separate eligibility paths within one administration model | Its reading volumes and advancement threshold |
| Madrasati, WSIS 2025 | Award-winning Saudi education platform | Evidence-led submission, reach and usability | LMS breadth or assumed NBC-scale traffic |
| GCC Digital Government Award, 2025 | Explicit public-service award criteria | UX, governance, performance and impact | Treating award criteria as an NBC tender rubric |

## 1. وطني الحبيب: close thematic precedent

On **11 September 2014**, SPA reported King Abdulaziz Public Library’s student competition with **28 questions** about Saudi history, cultural landmarks, economic resources and public figures. Its stated educational purpose included belonging and encouraging reading. A separate SPA report records a winner announcement and prize presentation, without a usable named-winner list in the evidence reviewed. [SPA launch](https://www.spa.gov.sa/1271668?lang=ar&newsid=1271668), [SPA award event](https://www.spa.gov.sa/1274098).

**Proposal lesson:** show a committee reviewer exactly why each question belongs in the bank and where its approved source is recorded.

**Limit:** neither its scoring method nor timer, tie rule or platform supplier was verified. It is not a prior edition of NBC. Its broad general-knowledge content does not expand NBC’s single-book question scope.

## 2. تحدي القراءة العربي: explicit judging and named winners

The official judging guide allocates **20 marks** to presenting a book and identifying its main idea, with distinct performance descriptions for grades 1–6 and 7–12. It also assesses lessons learned and connections to real life. This is an undated guide inspected on 7 September 2026. [Official judging guide, p. 1](https://arabreadingchallenge.com/ar/downloadPDF/72/1soJMCfTeGqewsrbBvqPnROEUyqGvfQwjdvJXIkd.pdf).

Dubai’s Government Media Office announced three first-place winners on **23 October 2024**: **حاتم محمد جاسم التركاوي**, **كادي بنت مسفر الخثعمي**, and **سلسبيل حسن صوالحة**. Each received AED 500,000. [Official 2024 results](https://mediaoffice.ae/ar/news/2024/october/23-10/mohammed-bin-rashid-honours-arab-reading-challeng-winners).

**Proposal lesson:** make rules explicit and represent the organizer’s actual award categories faithfully.

**Limit:** the undated guide is not proven to be the exact rubric used in 2024. Shared first places do not establish equal scores or reveal a tie-resolution mechanism. NBC does not authorize us to introduce shared prizes, subjective judging or multiple books.

## 3. إثراء أقرأ: the experience should serve reading

Ithra’s **2024 annual report**, printed pp. 96–97, describes iRead and its Reader of the Year award in terms of written description and critical reception of books. Its 2024 final-event page documents a judging presentation stage. [Ithra annual report](https://www.ithra.com/application/files/1817/4705/0715/Annual_Report_Eng.pdf), [official judging-event page](https://www.ithra.com/ar/programme/2024/iread-final-event/final-event-programs/iread-final-presentations).

**Proposal lesson:** present the book as a usable reading resource throughout the journey. Completion of an electronic form alone does not demonstrate the educational purpose.

**Limit:** the event page was available in the delegated research but failed during coordinator retrieval. A possible winner name appeared in an image in the annual report; it is deliberately omitted here because the coordinator did not visually verify that image. No individual judging breakdown or causal explanation of the winning work was established.

## 4. المهارات الثقافية: a strong Saudi operational reference

The Ministry of Culture’s competition portal describes its Ministry of Education partnership and allows private and international schools. The live short-story rubric gives middle/secondary entries **30% technical proficiency, 35% storytelling/communication, and 35% impact**. [Official competition portal](https://engage.moc.gov.sa/cst/), [short-story rubric](https://engage.moc.gov.sa/cst/cst-short-story/).

The first-season winner archive names **رهف عصام الأهدل** for **محكمة الحيوانات**, middle-school first place, and **محمد نسيم آل ابراهيم** for **رقصة الأرض**, secondary first place in the short-story track. [Official first-season results](https://engage.moc.gov.sa/cst/s1-winners-page/).

**Proposal lesson:** demonstrate correct stage selection, inclusion of school types, and a controlled path from participation to approved results. Give administrators a coherent model instead of disconnected exports.

**Limit:** these are creative-competition weights. The currently published rubric is not verified as the first-season rubric, and the source does not reveal individual scores or prove why one work beat another. Its public gallery does not authorize publishing NBC participants’ personal details.

## 5. المشروع الوطني للقراءة: school and university journeys

The Egyptian National Reading Programme publishes separate **الطالب المثقف** and **الجامعي المثقف** processes. Its school rules assess comprehension and connections to reality and use an **85%** minimum in specified advancement stages. The winner archive lists **ياسمين محمد مصطفى** among school winners and **نرمين محمد**, Faculty of Medicine, Beni Suef University, among university winners; the extracted evidence does not establish their individual ranks. [School rules](https://nationalreadingprogramme.com/الطالب-المثقف/), [university rules](https://nationalreadingprogramme.com/الجامعي-المثقف/), [winner archive](https://nationalreadingprogramme.com/الفائزون/).

**Proposal lesson:** show how different education pathways feed consistent reporting and administration.

**Limit:** this is an Egyptian, multi-book, multi-stage program. Its eligibility, thresholds and workload do not transfer to NBC. Publication dates on the consulted live pages were not established.

## 6. Madrasati: an actual digital-platform award

ITU’s **7 July 2025** announcement identifies **Madrasati Learning Management System**, Saudi Ministry of Education, as the WSIS 2025 winner in e-learning. This independently corroborates the Ministry’s report. [ITU official winners](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2025-07-07-WSIS-opening.aspx).

The Ministry describes its submission preparation: studying award criteria, choosing qualified projects, and preparing forms and supporting evidence before the application and voting stages. It reports a service reaching over six million students and more than half a million teachers. Those are the Ministry’s historical reported scale figures, not independently audited figures or an NBC capacity forecast. [Ministry of Education award report](https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/MOEnews/Pages/news1_07072025.aspx).

**Proposal lesson:** prepare proof alongside the narrative. Demonstrate the required journey, measured usability, reliable submission and realistic support.

**Limit:** an award for an operating national platform is different from selecting a contractor for a future service. Neither source establishes a vendor bidding method or proves that a specific interface style caused the win.

## 7. GCC Digital Government Award: closer evidence about digital-service criteria

The GCC Secretariat’s announcement dated **20 July 2025** lists four criteria for best government digital service: innovation/service development, technology and governance, user experience, and performance/achieved impact. Its purposeful-game category separately includes educational value, interaction/design and social/knowledge impact. [Official award criteria](https://www.gcc-sg.org/ar/MediaCenter/Announcements/Pages/DigitalAward2025.aspx).

**Proposal lesson:** balance the visual concept with an evidence plan for service quality, operational responsibility and outcomes.

**Limit:** NBC’s brief does not identify this award or adopt its criteria. No weighting should be transferred. The separate Saudi DGA award guide could be found in search results but returned 403 on direct access; this collection uses the readable GCC primary source instead.

## What we can honestly say about “what wins”

The evidence shows published winners, explicit criteria in several comparators, and one documented platform-award preparation process. It does not provide controlled evidence that any single feature causes victory.

Our recommendation is therefore to maximize **brief fidelity, demonstrable quality and evaluator confidence**. A competitor may show an attractive landing page; we can show that landing page plus a safe return to an interrupted attempt, a reproducible result and an approved publication process. This is our strategic hypothesis, to be tested against NBC’s actual rubric when obtained.
