# Ideas and differentiators

Parent: [[00-NBC-Research-Index]]

> [!abstract] Selection rule
> Prefer ideas that make a requirement easier to understand, operate or verify. An idea is useful when its benefit can be demonstrated and its scope fits the competition.

## Priority definitions

**Baseline:** required by the supplied brief. **Strengthener:** our recommended way to deliver a baseline well. **Optional:** requires a scope decision and may wait. **Avoid:** conflicts with known rules or creates an unjustified burden. Effort labels below are relative planning judgments, not estimates.

## Recommended shortlist

| Idea | Class | Benefit to demonstrate | Effort | Dependency / caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Book access beside the question journey | Baseline + strengthener | Read and return without losing selections | Medium | Approved accessible book and redistribution rights |
| Save status and recoverable attempt | Strengthener | Participant understands whether work is safe | Medium | Approved resume, security-session and closing policy |
| Committee source trace for every item | Strengthener | Reviewer can verify the question against the approved edition | Medium | Scientific review workflow and source file |
| Fairness preview before launch | Strengthener | Check coverage, form assignments and tied-score behavior | Medium | Approved question model; not a claim of psychometric equivalence |
| Results approval workspace | Strengthener | Separate calculation, exceptions and publication | Medium | Named decision authority |
| Reconciled stage/locality reporting | Baseline + strengthener | Reliable counts with clear denominators | Medium | Resolve locality hierarchy and completion definitions |
| Quiet reminder policy | Baseline + strengthener | Remind incomplete entrants without excessive alerts | Low–medium | Approved cadence, costs and communication rules |
| Arabic usability evidence pack | Strengthener | Show observed improvements and resolved errors | Medium | Participants, accessibility expertise and representative tasks |
| Restore demonstration | Strengthener | Prove recoverability of a known dataset | Medium | Approved hosting and recovery targets |

These priorities derive from [[02-Requirements-and-Acceptance-Matrix]] and our analysis. They are not a list of features observed in a winning NBC bid.

## Optional ideas worth exploring

### A searchable, accessible reading edition

An accessible text view, book outline and search could improve reading on smaller screens. Preserve approved wording and page references; retain the authorized original download. Search must not become an answer recommendation engine. Obtain conversion rights and committee validation before promising it.

**Evidence to seek:** participants can locate a section and return to their attempt without confusion. Measure the task rather than assuming an HTML version is always easier.

### A committee rehearsal mode

Allow authorized staff to rehearse campaign settings, question assignments, scoring, reporting and result release with synthetic data. Keep rehearsal completely separate from real participation.

**Evidence to seek:** staff can discover an incorrect setting before launch and correct it without affecting production records.

### Aggregate participation map

A private operator map may show broad participation and gaps, complementing required locality reports. Do not infer local attitudes or patriotism from participation or scores. Small-area counts may reveal individuals; use an approved suppression/aggregation policy. A public map is a separate publication decision.

**Evidence to seek:** administrators can answer a real operational question faster than with the existing table. If not, retain the simpler report.

### A post-campaign learning summary

An approved public summary could describe the book’s themes and the campaign’s reach. It must not disclose prohibited answer keys, imply unmeasured learning gains or expose student identities. The PDF’s no-answer-disclosure rule does not automatically expire at campaign closure.

**Evidence to seek:** committee-approved content and a clearly defined audience, publication policy and purpose.

### Administrative assistance using AI

Possible future uses include flagging inconsistent draft wording or helping staff search approved support material. Human review must retain authority over content and decisions. No personal data or live question bank should be sent to external AI services without approved processing arrangements.

**Evidence to seek:** measured reduction in editorial work without loss of accuracy or confidentiality. AI is optional; it is not required by the brief or supported as an NBC winning criterion.

## Ideas to avoid in the baseline

| Idea | Why it is unsuitable now |
|---|---|
| Speed leaderboard or fast-answer bonus | Directly conflicts with untimed answering and no time-based tie-breaking |
| “First to submit wins” | Uses time to decide outcomes |
| Forced forward-only questions | Conflicts with returning to previous questions |
| Closed-book restrictions or tab-switch penalties | Conflicts with permitted book access and creates false cheating signals |
| Immediate correct-answer explanations | Conflicts with the post-test non-disclosure rule |
| Public full names, IDs, grades and locality by default | Unnecessary disclosure; publication policy is unspecified |
| Camera proctoring, biometric checks or compulsory ID photos | Adds intrusive collection and scope absent from the brief |
| Adaptive difficulty without an approved comparability model | Can undermine a common highest-score ranking |
| AI tutor answering live scored questions | Changes what the competition measures and risks leaking answers |
| Heavy animation or a 3D homepage before core journeys | Adds delivery cost without evidence of evaluation value |

## Candidate concept statement

**منصة معرفة وانتماء** is a possible descriptive phrase for exploration, not a selected product name or cleared trademark. The working project label remains NBC_web until the organizer approves naming.

A useful distinguishing feature is the **committee’s ability to explain a result**: open an approved attempt, see its assigned form and scoring version, examine an exception, and release the decision with an audit record. Pair that operational strength with a calm, readable participant experience.

## Prioritization exercise

For each idea, record the source requirement, user problem, evidence needed, owner, dependency, relative effort and a condition for dropping it. Discuss the top three with the organizer before expanding scope. Prioritize the approved book experience, recoverable participation and controlled results; adjust when the actual evaluation rubric arrives.
