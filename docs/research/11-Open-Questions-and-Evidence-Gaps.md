# Open questions and evidence gaps

Parent: [[00-NBC-Research-Index]]

> [!important] Why this matters
> These questions identify missing inputs that could change the bid, cost or competition fairness. They are a ready-to-use discovery agenda, not a claim that the organizer omitted them from every document.

## Registration, communication and reporting

- The registration section asks for **region, governorate and village/center or equivalent**, while reports also refer to **cities**. Agree a geographic data dictionary and mapping.
- Included institution types are specified, but the registration fields do not explicitly require institution name or identifier. Determine whether eligibility is self-declared, verified through another system or checked by another approved method.
- A four-part name is required. Agree how to support valid legal names that do not fit a rigid four-box assumption.
- Determine whether a primary phone can be shared by siblings and how number changes are verified.
- Clarify whether the optional backup phone belongs to the participant or another person, and its permitted contact/recovery role.
- Define “complete” and “incomplete” consistently: registered-not-started, started, submitted, withdrawn, disqualified and duplicate records may need different treatment.
- Set reminder cadence, message channels, suppression rules, send limits and billing ownership.
- Decide the exact public winner fields and whether broader results remain private.
- Specify export formats, authorized roles and expected report sign-off.
- Confirm support dates/hours, escalation contacts, incident remedies and service targets.

These are derived gaps in the supplied document, not verified deficiencies in the eventual organizer process. [[Source-Committee-PDF]].

## Research stopping decision

The initial evidence is sufficient to guide preparation and avoid the main rule conflicts. More generic searches are unlikely to reveal the private invitation, final question bank, actual budget or committee decisions. The next useful step is obtaining those specific artifacts, not treating missing web results as proof of absence.

The collection should be updated when new source material arrives. Record what changed, which requirements are affected, and whether proposed designs or estimates need revision.
