# Sources and research method

Parent: [[00-NBC-Research-Index]]

> [!note] Evidence date
> Public-source retrieval was performed on 7 September 2026; synthesis and file verification continued on 8 September. Publication dates below belong to the source, not to our access date. An undated live rubric is not automatically the rubric used for a historical winner.

## Evidence hierarchy

The supplied PDF is the primary evidence for requested platform behavior, within the limits of an unverified final approval status. Official organizer records establish comparator rules and winners; regulator and standards sources establish their own requirements or guidance. Retail metadata is a bibliographic lead. Recommendations, design concepts, effort judgments, scorecard weights and future acceptance scenarios are our synthesis.

**Direct** means readable source text was retrieved, not that every linked page or interface was audited. **Indexed** means the search tool supplied official-source text while direct access was empty or blocked. Delegated evidence is identified where it could not be independently retrieved by the coordinator. Empty direct SPA pages did not erase the readable official search evidence.

## Search coverage and reconciliation

Research covered the exact Arabic competition title, the named book, the apparent organizer, SPA records, and an Etimad-focused public search; Saudi national-identity and cultural quizzes; Arab reading competitions and winner archives; digital-service awards; Arabic design and accessibility; Saudi privacy/cybersecurity; assessment fairness and identity assurance.

Two bounded research lanes examined comparators and cultural/design/safeguard sources. The coordinator reviewed the supplied PDF, investigated exact competition identity, checked consequential comparator and regulatory records, and synthesized the documents. No accounts were registered and no messages were sent to the organizer.

Key reconciliations:

- Same-title lectures/forums were not labeled earlier competition editions.
- Retail page-count disagreement was preserved; no approved book contents were invented.
- Historical winners were not paired with undated rubrics as if the correspondence were proven.
- Student judging criteria and digital awards were not represented as supplier procurement rules.
- PDPL, NCA, DGA guidance and W3C/NIST references were distinguished by scope and authority.
- A source that reported reach was not treated as an independent impact evaluation.

## Excluded or incomplete evidence

The organizer homepage could not be retrieved. The Saudi DGA award-guide PDF returned 403; readable GCC award criteria were used instead. Full DGA accessibility and Vision-plan retrieval were unavailable. UNESCO direct pages presented access challenges. A live visual inspection of the Ministry of Culture portal timed out. These limitations are recorded in the relevant notes.

No verified exact NBC tender, supplier winner, prior competition results, current opening/closing dates, budget, final evaluation weights, approved book master or question bank was found. No participant interviews, benchmark usability audit, software build, load test or legal compliance audit was performed.

## Why research stops here

The evidence is sufficient for a grounded initial proposal strategy and the known operational requirements are mapped. Remaining high-impact gaps chiefly require specific organizer documents and decisions. Repeating broad searches would be unlikely to resolve them. The research is complete for the available material, with those gaps explicitly open in [[11-Open-Questions-and-Evidence-Gaps]].

## Maintaining this collection

### Verification performed

The final collection passed structural checks for required metadata, heading order, balanced code fences, all 47 requirement IDs, source-ledger coverage, 114 internal links and the preserved PDF checksum. The 15 reader-facing notes are linked from the index. All four source-PDF pages were visually reviewed. Obsidian reading-view and Mermaid rendering were not available for visual verification; the Markdown received structural review instead.

### Updating the evidence

When new evidence arrives, record its publisher, version, date, retrieval method and authority. Update the affected requirement, design or estimate; keep proposed assumptions separate from confirmed decisions. Recheck live rules and regulatory versions before final bid or implementation. Internal audit records reside in the collection’s evidence folder; reader-facing notes are the indexed Markdown files.
