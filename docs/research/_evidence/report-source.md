# NBC platform proposal: canonical research synthesis

Parent: [[00-NBC-Research-Index]]

## Audience, scope and evidence date

This synthesis is for the NBC_web team preparing to win the platform proposal/build competition. It excludes student strategies for winning the knowledge quiz. Scope is the supplied 1448/2026 committee brief, Saudi institutional and cultural context, related historical competitions, relevant design and safeguard references, and preparation decisions before development. Public retrieval occurred on 7 September 2026; synthesis was finalized on 8 September.

## Executive answer

Prepare an Arabic competition service whose quality can be demonstrated through fidelity to the approved rules, accessible book use, controlled assessment and dependable operation. Obtain the actual supplier evaluation pack before optimizing the submission or committing to implementation. This is a strategic recommendation, not an official rubric or promise of first place.

The supplied PDF is the controlling evidence for this research baseline: objective book-based questions; random distribution; backward navigation; book access/download; untimed answering; automatic scoring; highest-score winners; no time-based tie-break; no post-test correct-answer disclosure; administrative reports, notifications, privacy, backups and support. It was visually read in full and normalized into 47 entries. [[Source-Committee-PDF]], [[02-Requirements-and-Acceptance-Matrix]].

## Evidence-supported implications

Historical same-title SPA records establish institutional context but do not establish a prior competition edition or platform award. The approved book remains unavailable; Samawy’s same-title entry provides only a bibliographic lead with conflicting page counts. [[01-Competition-and-Book-Intelligence]].

The strongest comparator evidence includes official reading and cultural rubrics, named winners, ITU’s confirmation of Madrasati’s 2025 e-learning award, and published GCC digital-service criteria. None identifies NBC supplier weights or a causal recipe for winning. [ITU winners](https://www.itu.int/en/mediacentre/Pages/PR-2025-07-07-WSIS-opening.aspx), [GCC criteria](https://www.gcc-sg.org/ar/MediaCenter/Announcements/Pages/DigitalAward2025.aspx), [[03-Comparable-Competitions-and-Winners]].

Assessment design needs explicit decisions about equivalent forms, fixed assignments, scoring versions, corrections, ties and publication. ETS supports the general concern about comparing forms, not a claim that any proposed NBC bank is calibrated. [ETS equating abstract](https://www.ets.org/research/policy_research_reports/publications/report/2010/ilrs.html), [[06-Assessment-Fairness-and-Content]].

An appropriate safeguard plan depends on the actual data flows and parties. PDPL, its regulation, NCA scope and external accessibility/authentication references must not be collapsed into an unsupported compliance claim. [[07-Privacy-Security-and-Operations]].

## Recommendations and deliverable organization

Explore a book-led contemporary institutional design; compare a restrained woven-line concept; validate the Arabic journey, error recovery and accessibility. Prepare an evidence-oriented demo, an internal readiness scorecard, transparent scope/cost drivers and a handover plan. These are proposed future activities. [[05-Arabic-Design-and-Experience]], [[08-Proposal-and-Winning-Strategy]].

The reader-facing collection separates source intelligence, requirements, comparators, culture, design, fairness, safeguards, proposal strategy, ideas, discovery, gaps, sources and an Arabic brief. The claim-source ledger is the single structured record of source title, publisher, date, URL, access limitations and supported claim.

## Material limitations and stopping rationale

No verified exact tender, vendor winner, prior edition, budget, submission calendar, official supplier rubric, approved book master or question bank was obtained. Several official sources were available only as indexed excerpts. No stakeholder interviews, live visual benchmark audit, software implementation or compliance certification was performed. These are explicit limits of completed research, not filled with assumptions.

Further general searching is unlikely to resolve organizer-controlled information. The next high-value work is the targeted discovery agenda in [[11-Open-Questions-and-Evidence-Gaps]], followed by validating the actual selection criteria and delivery constraints.
