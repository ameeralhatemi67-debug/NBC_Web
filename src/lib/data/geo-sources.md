# Analytics map data

The map shows registration counts at representative city points. It does not show participant addresses or population density.

City centers: GeoNames SA.zip retrieved 2026-10-14 from https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/SA.zip . WGS84 latitude/longitude. GeoNames attribution and CC BY 4.0 license: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Each GeoNames point retains its ID. Al-Ahsa uses Al-Hufuf as its representative populated-place center. Al-Bayda uses the map link published by the Eastern Province Emirate at https://www.sharqiah.gov.sa/governorates/al-baidhah : 26°21'40.9"N 49°58'10.7"E. Converted to decimal degrees: 26.361361,49.969639. It is not assigned an unrelated sand/desert feature with the same name. New registration cities without a verified center remain in totals and comparisons and are explicitly listed as unmapped.
